# PixelGuard Privacy Policy

PixelGuard processes selected files entirely inside your browser. Images, watermark assets, presets, and export data are not uploaded to PixelGuard or any third party by this application. PixelGuard does not include telemetry, analytics, tracking pixels, or user accounts in this static build.

Presets are stored only in your browser's local storage and can be removed by deleting browser site data. If a buyer adds payment, analytics, or hosting integrations, those services must publish their own disclosures.
